A Mortgage Backed icon next to your apps that opens the game full screen and buzzes you on your turn. It's a shortcut to this site, not an App Store app.

  3. Open Mortgage Backed from your Home Screen Within 10 minutes. It opens your game, already signed in.
  4. Turn on alerts Tap the bell at the top of the game, then Turn on alerts.
