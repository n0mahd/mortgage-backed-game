Turn alerts sheet, iPhone Safari

Turn alerts

Get a buzz when it's your turn and the game isn't open. On iPhone, that needs the game on your Home Screen.

Adding it puts a Mortgage icon next to your apps that opens this game full screen. It's a shortcut to the site, not an App Store app, so there's nothing to download.

Add to Home Screen steps

  1. Tap ShareThe square with an arrow in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Tap Add to Home ScreenScroll down the list if you don't see it, then tap Add.
  3. Open Mortgage from your Home ScreenWithin 10 minutes. It opens your game, already signed in.
  4. Turn on alertsTap the bell at the top of the game, then Turn on alerts.
