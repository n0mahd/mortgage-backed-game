Add the game to your Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open the new Mortgage icon within 10 minutes. You'll already be signed in.
  4. In the game, tap Alerts, then Turn on alerts.

Leave this page open until you've added it.
